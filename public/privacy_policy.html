DaVinci Decoders Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 6, 2026

1. Overview

DaVinci Decoders (https://pnowell.github.io/codebusters/) is an educational Science Olympiad cipher and cryptogram practice web application. Students can practice freely in Guest Mode without signing in, or sign in with a Google account if they are members of an authorized Science Olympiad classroom roster.

2. Information Accessed via Google Sign-In

When you voluntarily click Sign In and authenticate with Google (via Google Firebase Authentication), DaVinci Decoders accesses only standard OpenID Connect identity fields:

  • Email address — used strictly to check whether your account is on the coach-managed classroom allowlist.
  • Google Account Unique Identifier (UID) — used to associate your saved cipher progress, leaderboard scores, and practice test attempts with your account.
  • Basic profile display name — used only as a fallback if no classroom roster nickname is assigned.

DaVinci Decoders does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Contacts, Calendar, or any other sensitive or restricted Google API scopes.

3. How Information Is Used and Stored

  • Guest Mode (No Sign-In): Drill settings, mastery progress, and completed quote IDs are stored solely in your own browser's localStorage and are never transmitted to a server.
  • Authorized Classroom Accounts: If your email address is on the classroom allowlist, your assigned display name, cipher mastery statistics, completed quote history, drill high scores, and timed practice test responses are stored in Google Cloud Firestore so your progress syncs across devices and appears on classroom team leaderboards and coach review views.
  • Unlisted Accounts: If you sign in with a Google account that is not on the classroom allowlist, no cloud profile or Firestore user record is created, and the app continues operating locally in Guest Mode.

4. Data Sharing & Google API Limited Use Disclosure

DaVinci Decoders does not sell, rent, or share Google user data with advertisers, data brokers, or any external third parties, and does not use Google user data for artificial intelligence or machine learning model training.

Within an authorized classroom team, your coach-assigned display name and drill/test scores are visible to fellow rostered teammates (for example, on team leaderboards and partner practice tests) and to the team coach. Infrastructure hosting is provided by Google Firebase / Google Cloud Platform and GitHub Pages.

DaVinci Decoders' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data Security

All communication between your browser, Google Authentication, and Cloud Firestore is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS and encrypted at rest by Google Cloud. Server-side Firestore Security Rules enforce strict role-based access control so only verified classroom roster members and the team coach can read or write classroom records.

6. Data Retention, Deletion & Contact

Guest Mode data can be cleared at any time by clearing site data in your web browser. Authorized classroom students or parents may request changes to their display name or complete deletion of their cloud profile, practice history, and test records at any time by contacting their team coach or opening a request on the project repository at github.com/pnowell/codebusters/issues.
